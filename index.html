01 / ABOUT

Engineering is not only about structures. It is about the places people live in.

My academic path combines civil engineering with environmental thinking. I’m especially interested in how urbanization changes land, vegetation and the relationship between built environments and ecological systems.

My goal is to contribute to sustainable urban development by balancing human progress with environmental preservation—using analysis, mapping and practical engineering knowledge to turn complex problems into clearer decisions.

“Meaningful development should leave room for both people and nature.”
